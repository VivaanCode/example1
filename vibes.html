A History of Sweetness

Cake has been a centerpiece of human celebration for centuries. Originally evolving from simple sweetened breads, modern cakes were made possible by the discovery of refined ingredients, precise baking techniques, and reliable leavening agents.

Today, whether it's a grand multi-tiered wedding cake or a simple homemade loaf for a Tuesday evening craving, cake represents joy, sharing, and culinary creativity. Every slice tells a story of tradition and flavor.
